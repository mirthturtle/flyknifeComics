<p class="dramatic">
I will tell you of a time of valour.<br />
Of angels and demons.<br />
Of battles beyond all imagining.<br />
I will tell you of the Zap,<br />
For its tale is the tale of all humanity.</p>

Blasting directly into your brains from the 1989 that never was, _The Valiant Stars_ is the snakepunk adventure you never knew existed and never thought you needed! In a time beyond time, among stars beyond space, the nascent human empire is already in decline. 200 years ago, insectoid cyborgs called the Kozark invaded and nearly exterminated humanity- it would've been the finish line for the human race but for the mysterious and short-lived intervention of psychic aliens called the Zybo! Now, the profound generational scar left by this unprecedented tragedy still haunts humanity. Their mightiest defenders? Fifty rejects, dweebs, and three-time losers called the **ZAP JOCKEYS**. Granted incredible, death-defying superpowers by 200 years of training in an alien megastructure called the Pyre Cube, they stand ready to protect Earth from another invasion. But their far remove from the humanity they seek to defend has made them aliens in their own right, and trust is a hard thing to come by in space.

Begin your journey in the far-flung future of 2014 and join **SNAKES LENINGRAD** and **CAPTAIN GUNSMOKE** for a whirlwind adventure featuring alien planets, impossible landscapes, powerful friends, and nightmarish foes! Live the saga alongside **COMMANDER JOACHIM PEGASUS**, framed for a murder he didn't commit, set up to fall by powers that gaze on Earth with covetous eyes! Witness **FOX BURKE**, forced to confront her evil clone, still in the service of a dying alien empire! With _The Valiant Stars_, you have a front-row seat to the Battle for Ghiscard, the Life Comis 2014 Chilled-Out Summertime Jam, the Second Battle of Earth, and beyond!

Read on for a story of comedy, exhilaration, radical skateboarding, "fast snacks", laser beams, proton bombs, conspiracies, tragedies, gods, monsters, and the redemptive power of love!

<p class="dramatic">It's a time desperate for heroes!</p>

  <summary>THE VALIANT STARS Backstage Experience</summary>
  <a href="/gallery/starbreakers2"><img data-float="left" src="" /></a>
  <p>In 2025, I started drawing characters based on miniatures that I painted in my downtime. This was a project to diversify my character designs, imagine new stories, and make myself practice new tools and techniques. After a while I wasn't satisfied just drawing the characters so I started adding story blurbs; eventually that didn't satisfy either so I decided every character would get their own strip to go with. These were mostly knocked out very fast, usually with the intention of practicing one specific thing and just getting the characters 'installed' in my head for when I wanted to draw them in my more serious work. These characters and strips are collected and presented here, with out-of-character commentary by me (Bruce, the author/artist).</p>
</details>
